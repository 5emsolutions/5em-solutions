What we do

The name says repair and paint. The work is bigger than that.

We frame decks and porches, tear off and replace roofs including commercial flat roofs, take rooms down to the joists and rebuild them, and cut rot out of the side of your house before it becomes a framing problem. Painting is part of what we do, not all of it.
