What we run into on Nashville houses

Most of what we remodel in Nashville was built between about 1972 and 1995 — brick ranches and split-levels in the neighborhoods off Eastern Avenue and out toward Stoney Creek. Solid houses. Good framing. Kitchens with soffits above the cabinets, laminate counters, and a layout that made sense when the kitchen was a room you closed the door on.

Opening those kitchens up is our most requested job here, and it is one where the honest answer varies a lot house to house. Sometimes the wall between the kitchen and the den is not carrying anything and it is a two-day job. Sometimes it is carrying the ceiling joists and you need a beam, a header, and an engineer’s letter. We find out which before we quote, not after we open it.

The other thing worth knowing about houses of this vintage: the drywall is often 1/2 inch over 16-inch centers with a heavy knockdown or popcorn texture. Matching that texture on a repair is the difference between a job you notice and one you do not. We hang and finish our own drywall specifically so that texture match is our problem and not a subcontractor’s.

Most common calls in Nashville

  • Seventies and eighties kitchens with cabinet soffits and closed-off layouts
  • Removing or opening a wall — and finding out first whether it is load-bearing
  • Popcorn and knockdown texture matching on drywall repairs
  • Flooring over subfloor that needs leveling before anything goes down
  • Bathroom and laundry floors that have gone soft around the fixtures

Nashville questions

Can you take out the wall between my kitchen and living room?

Usually. Whether it is a two-day job or a two-week job depends entirely on whether that wall is holding the ceiling up. We open a small section and look before we give you a price, so the number you get is the number you pay.

How long will my kitchen be out of use?

A cosmetic refresh is one to two weeks. A full gut with new cabinets, counters and floors is usually five to eight weeks, and most of the waiting is countertop fabrication, not us. We will give you a real schedule and tell you when it slips.
